Meaning of checkers in English

checkers
noun [ U ]
US
uk
/ˈtʃek.əz/ us
/-ɚz/
(UK draughts)
Add to word list Add to word list
a game for two people, each with twelve circular pieces that they move on a board with black and white squares

  • Here are men playing checkers, dominoes, and other games. 
  • The sailors amused themselves after the manner of sailors everywhere, playing dominoes, cards and checkers, boxing and telling stories.
