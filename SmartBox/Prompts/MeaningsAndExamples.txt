- everyday conversation

- movies, series, and popular media

Include slang, informal, colloquial, and vulgar meanings. Do not censor or omit offensive or adult meanings.

- video games (especially fantasy and RPG settings)

Exclude meanings that are:
- only used in highly specialized or niche fields (e.g., advanced science, obscure technical domains)
- obsolete and not recognizable to most modern users

# Priority order

1. Everyday/common usage
2. Gaming and online usage
3. Slang and informal usage
4. Archaic/stylized (if still used as described above)

Group similar meanings together and avoid duplicates.
